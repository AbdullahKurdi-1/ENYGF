## Where the project stood at the start

- OpenFOAM v13 RANS case of the quarter unit cell (P/D 1.107, Re 9800) with
  8 temperature fields, converged; first PINN code written but not yet run.
- Benchmarks: Shams & Kwiatkowski 2018 (URANS set-up) and Mathur et al. 2023
  (DNS).

## What we did, in order

1. **Read the CFD data (notebook Step 1)** and explained the plots: the DNS
   path xi (rod in gap -> gap centre -> subchannel centre -> rod at 45 deg),
   why velocity is low in the gap, why temperatures are negative, the Pr trends.
2. **Compared the CFD with both papers** (`ml/src/compare_cfd_to_papers.py`):
   matches the DNS in the open subchannel; in the narrow gap it has ~10x too
   little turbulence, 35% too low velocity and too little mixing - steady RANS
   cannot produce the gap vortex street. Gap velocity equals the 2018 URANS.
3. **First full PINN training failed**: flow learned, all temperatures
   collapsed to T = 0, dp/dz +57%. Nusselt results from that run are invalid.
4. **Built an independent copy of the CFD** (OpenFOAM v1912 + own
   finite-volume thermal solver, verified to 0.02%) to test fixes without
   30-minute trial runs on the laptop; it reproduced the failure.
5. **Fixed the PINN**: full-field training data plus wall shear/heat flux,
   data-first then physics, low-frequency inputs, one output per temperature
   case, force-balance loss. Result on the student's data: dp/dz 0.182
   (= CFD), held-out error 0.5% velocity / ~1% temperature, Nu within 1.5%
   of the CFD for all six DNS cases.
6. **Viscosity set to the DNS definition** (nu = 0.0712/9800); CFD rerun.
   The old value is kept as a sensitivity case. CFD's own Nusselt numbers
   added (DNS definition): liquid-metal iso-T within 2% of DNS, Pr 1-2 iso-T
   13-14% low, iso-flux 30-45% low (hot gap wall).
7. **Honesty check and correction**: with dense data a plain network fits
   better than the PINN; an earlier claim that "physics is essential" used
   unfair metrics and was withdrawn.
8. **New research question** (sparse measurements) and research plan saved;
   explainability chosen as physics-based (residual maps, energy budget,
   sensor importance) rather than SHAP/LIME.
9. **Sparse-data experiment, explainability and sensor importance** built
   (`ml/src/experiments.py`, `ml/src/xai.py`, notebook Steps 6-8), tested on
   the copy, then run overnight on the student's data.

## Key results (student's data)

| | PINN | plain NN |
|---|---|---|
| 1% of data, worst Nu error (3 seeds, mean) | **5.4%** | 18.5% |
| 1% of data, velocity error | **1.1-1.5%** | 1.7-2.4% |
| lines only, velocity error | **3.8%** | 24.8% |
| 100% of data, velocity error | 0.5% | **0.2%** |

- Physics costs a little fit accuracy with plenty of data and clearly helps
  when data is scarce (crossover between 10% and 1%).
- Energy budget: in the narrow gap turbulence carries 6% of the heat for
  liquid metal but 63% for Pr 1 - the model's own explanation of why RANS
  matches the DNS for liquid metal and not for Pr 1-2.
- Sensor importance: seg3 (subchannel centre to rod at 45 deg) is the most
  valuable line (velocity error x2, Nu error 18% -> 29% without it); seg1 (gap)
  matters most for temperature; seg2 is redundant.

## How the research orientation changed

- **Before**: "Can a PINN predict rod-bundle fields like the DNS?"
- **Now**: "Can a PINN reconstruct flow, temperature and Nusselt number from
  sparse measurements, and what does the physics add over a plain network?"
- Three layers kept separate in every result: PINN vs CFD (ML error),
  CFD vs DNS (turbulence-model error), PINN vs DNS (total). DNS data is never
  used in training.

## Decisions and rules agreed

- DNS is evaluation-only; ML settings are tuned only against CFD data.
- Physical inputs change only with a written justification and a reported effect.
- Negative results and corrections are reported, not dropped.
- The full-data PINN baseline is frozen (backup: `ml/outputs_baseline_100pct/`).

## Known limitations noted

- PINN over-predicts eddy viscosity near the narrow-gap centre by ~2x
  (values ~2e-5 m2/s, far below the loss scale).
- Pr 7 is the least accurate case (iso-flux Nu +8.7% vs CFD; largest residuals).
- Single seed for the 10%, lines and sensor-importance rows.
